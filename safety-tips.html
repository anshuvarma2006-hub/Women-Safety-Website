Safety Information

Stay Safe. Stay Aware.

Simple safety practices can help you stay prepared and make informed decisions.

🚶

Personal Safety

  • Stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Keep important emergency contacts easily accessible.
  • Inform a trusted person about your plans when appropriate.
  • Use well-known and safer routes whenever possible.
📱

Digital Safety

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Avoid sharing passwords or verification codes.
  • Check links before opening them.
  • Review privacy settings on online accounts.
🚌

Travel Safety

  • Plan your route before travelling.
  • Keep your phone charged when travelling.
  • Prefer trusted transportation options.
  • Keep important contact information available.
🚨

Emergency Preparedness

  • Keep emergency contact numbers available.
  • Know how to access your device's emergency features.
  • Keep trusted contacts updated.
  • If you are in immediate danger, seek help from appropriate emergency services.
💡

Quick Safety Reminder

Stay calm, remain aware of your surroundings, and contact a trusted person or appropriate emergency service when necessary.
